## 🚀 Featured projects

### 🪐 Orbital — gravity slingshot puzzle
Slingshot a probe through gravity wells and land it in the green ring. 10 hand-made levels, **each proven solvable by a brute-force solver**, an in-game level editor, and share links encoded entirely in the URL (no server).
`JavaScript` · `Canvas` · `MIT`
▶️ **[Play](https://arsam92.github.io/my-site/)** &nbsp;•&nbsp; 💻 **[Source](https://github.com/arsam92/my-site)**

### 🔥 ASHFALL — *in war, no one wins*
A narrative survival game about a city under siege. Assign survivors by day, face irreversible choices at dusk, risk night raids for supplies. Five interwoven storylines, permadeath, and multiple endings.
`HTML` · `JavaScript`
▶️ **[Play](https://arsam92.github.io/game/)**

### ⚽ Chaos Soccer — 2v2 ragdoll brawl
A physics-driven 2v2 soccer brawl with **23 abilities** and **4 players on one device** (keyboard, gamepad, or split-screen touch). No login, no backend, no paid APIs.
`HTML` · `Canvas`
▶️ **[Play](https://arsam92.github.io/igo-imo-ioi/)**

### ☀️ Solar System — first-person tour
An interactive first-person walkthrough (WASD + mouse) of the solar system with real planetary data and 12 achievements.
`HTML`
▶️ **[Play](https://arsam92.github.io/-solar/)**
